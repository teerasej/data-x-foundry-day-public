# Fabrikam Planned Maintenance Playbook

Document ID: `KB-MAINTENANCE-003`

This document contains synthetic training information only.

## Before maintenance

Publish a service notice at least three business days before a planned change. State the affected service, maintenance window, expected impact, and rollback owner.

## During maintenance

Record start time, current phase, and any deviation from the approved change. Do not expose internal credentials, customer data, or private diagnostic links.

## After maintenance

Confirm service health, close the public notice, and record follow-up actions. If validation fails, stop the change and ask the human change owner to decide whether to roll back.
