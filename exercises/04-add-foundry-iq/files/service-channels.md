# Fabrikam Service Support Channels

Document ID: `KB-CHANNELS-002`

This document contains synthetic training information only.

## Standard support

Use the Service Portal for ordinary incidents and requests. Include the affected service, a short symptom description, the time observed, and a non-sensitive screenshot when useful.

## Urgent support

Use the on-call escalation route only for a confirmed Severity 1 incident. A human reviewer decides whether the issue qualifies; an AI agent must not activate an on-call response by itself.

## Billing questions

Route duplicate charges, refund requests, and incorrect invoices to a human billing specialist. Do not promise a refund or state that a payment has been reversed.
